# 2-Minute Demo Script

## 0:00 — Problem

"Small factories have machine data, but AI systems are often cloud-first, expensive and uncomfortable for sensitive production data."

## 0:20 — Factory floor

Open FactoryPulse and show six machines.

Point to one machine marked CRITICAL.

"FactoryPulse continuously converts raw telemetry into machine health and maintenance priority."

## 0:40 — Explain the anomaly

Select the critical machine.

Show temperature, vibration, power and load.

"The important part is not just anomaly detection. We show the evidence and infer a likely cause."

## 1:00 — Energy intelligence

Open the energy table.

"At the same time, we identify machines whose power profile is above their local baseline and estimate potential waste."

## 1:20 — Edge story

Open Edge performance & privacy.

"This demo performs its anomaly analysis locally. No cloud AI is required."

Then say:

"For Phase 2, the AI explanation layer will run through the Kompact AI Runtime on CPU hardware, and we will report actual latency, memory and throughput measurements."

## 1:40 — Why FactoryPulse

"Instead of building another AI chatbot, we built an AI decision layer for a real operational problem where privacy, cost and local execution matter."

## 1:55 — Close

"FactoryPulse turns factory telemetry into an actionable maintenance and energy signal, while keeping the architecture ready for sovereign edge inference."
